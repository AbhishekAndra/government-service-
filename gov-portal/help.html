Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Use the chat assistant (bottom-right) or contact us.

How do I track my application?

Go to "Track Application" in the main menu and enter your Application ID (e.g. APP-88231). You'll see a full timeline of every action taken on it.

What documents can I upload?

PDF, JPG or PNG files up to 5 MB each. Every upload is automatically virus-scanned before being stored in your Document Vault.

How do I sign in without a smartphone?

Visit any Citizen Service Centre for biometric (fingerprint/iris) verification, or use username/password if you're a registered official.

Is my data secure?

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) and stored documents are encrypted at rest. See our Privacy Policy for details on data handling under the DPDP Act, 2023.

The site is in English — can I switch to Hindi?

Yes — use the language switch (EN / हिंदी) at the top-right of every page.
