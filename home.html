How it works

Apply for services online, step by step

  1. 1

    Select a service

    Search or browse for what you need.

  2. 2

    Register / sign in

    Staff sign in for verification; citizens apply directly.

  3. 3

    Submit application

    Fill in your details online.

  4. 4

    Upload documents

    Attach clear scans or photos as proof.

  5. 5

    Track status

    Follow progress anytime, no office visit needed.

  6. 6

    Receive approval

    Get your certificate or decision notice.
