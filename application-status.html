    What each status means

    Submitted
    Your application has been received and is in the queue for review.
    Processing
    An officer is checking your documents. No action needed from you yet.
    Approved
    Your request is complete. Look for a download link or collection notice.
    Rejected
    The reason is shown on your record. Most rejections can be corrected and resubmitted.

    Documents commonly requested

    • A government photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, or passport)
    • Proof of current address, dated within the last 3 months
    • Any certificate specific to the service (income, birth, business, etc.)
    • A recent passport-size photo, where the service asks for one

    Missing something? You can usually add it later from the Help page without restarting your application.

    Tracking questions

    My status hasn't changed in over a week — is that normal?
    Yes, if it's still within the estimated processing time shown above. Status usually only updates when your application moves to a new stage, not every day.
    I lost my reference number — can I still track my application?
    Check the confirmation email or SMS you received when you applied — it's included there. If you can't find it, contact support with your name and the service you applied for.
    Can I track more than one application at a time?
    Yes. Look up each reference number separately — one result is shown at a time, and you can search again immediately after.
    What happens after my application is approved?
    You'll see a download link or collection notice on this page, and a confirmation is sent to your registered email or number.
    Why was my application rejected?
    The specific reason is shown in your result above under the timeline. Most rejections are due to a missing or unclear document and can be corrected and resubmitted at no extra cost.
