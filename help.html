Common questions

Where can I find my reference number?
It is printed on your acknowledgement receipt and included in the confirmation message sent after you submit an application.
How long does document verification take?
Most submissions are reviewed within five working days. A service officer may contact you if more information is needed.
Can I edit an application after submission?
Use the contact form to ask for a correction. Include your reference number so the team can locate your record.
What documents do I need to scan or photograph?
Most services need a photo ID, an address proof, and any certificate specific to that service. Keep files under 5 MB in PDF, JPG, or PNG format.
Is there a fee to apply?
Most certificate and status services are free. Business registration and some licenses carry a fee, shown before you submit.
What if my application is rejected?
You'll see the reason on the Track application page. You can usually correct the issue and resubmit at no extra cost.
