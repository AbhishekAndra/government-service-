Public meetings & notices

Ward committees, public hearings, and district council sessions are open to residents. Sample listings below show the kind of notices you'd find here.

15

Ward Committee Meeting — Ward 12

6:00 PM · Community Hall, Ward 12 · Open to residents

Agenda: road maintenance budget, streetlight complaints, and waste collection schedule.

Sep

22

Public Hearing: Water Supply Expansion

11:00 AM · District Collectorate, Conference Hall

Residents can raise concerns about the proposed pipeline route before final approval.

Oct

03

District Council Budget Session

10:00 AM · District Council Chamber · Live-streamed

Quarterly review of district spending and upcoming infrastructure proposals.

Oct

10

Notice: Property Tax Assessment Review

Public notice period ends · Submit objections by this date

Property owners may formally contest new assessment values through the Revenue office.

How to attend or comment

  • • Most meetings are open to the public — no registration needed for ward and council sessions.
  • • Public hearings usually allow written comments submitted in advance through the district office.
  • • Can't attend in person? Many sessions are live-streamed — check the notice for a link closer to the date.
