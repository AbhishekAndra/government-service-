Before you travel

  • International visitors: check visa requirements well ahead of travel.
  • Domestic travellers: carry a valid photo ID for hotel check-ins and monument entry.
  • Some heritage sites require advance online ticket booking, especially on weekends.
  • Wildlife park safaris are limited in number per day — book early.
