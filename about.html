About the portal

One place for citizen services

The Citizen Services Portal brings together applications, certificates, and status tracking that used to require separate visits to different offices. Our goal is to make everyday government services simpler, faster, and easier to follow from anywhere.

Our mission

To reduce the time and effort citizens spend on paperwork by putting applications, verification, and support in a single, accessible portal — so that anyone, from any district, can access the same standard of service.

What we handle

  • Income and identity certificates
  • Address and identity corrections
  • Business registration and licensing
  • Pension and welfare scheme applications
  • Document upload and verification

Accessibility commitment

This portal is built to be usable with a keyboard alone, with screen readers, and at larger text sizes. We follow WCAG 2.1 AA guidance for contrast, focus visibility, and form labeling.

If you run into an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, please tell us using the support form — we treat these reports as a priority.

Milestones

  1. 2022

    Portal launched

    Income certificates and identity corrections became available online for the first time.

  2. 2023

    Document verification added

    Citizens could upload and track document verification without visiting an office.

  3. 2024

    Business & pension services

    Business registration and pension/welfare applications joined the portal.

  4. 2026

    Accessibility overhaul & new services

    Text-size controls, screen-reader support, and passport, land record, and scholarship services were added.
