Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account to apply?
No account is required for this demo — just fill in the application and keep your reference number to track it later.
Can I apply for more than one service at a time?
Yes. Each application gets its own reference number, so you can track them separately on the Track application page.
What file formats can I upload?
PDF, JPG, and PNG are accepted, up to 5 MB per file. Scan documents clearly and avoid glare or cropped edges.
Is my information kept private?
Your details are only used to process your application and are shared solely with the verifying officer.
Is there a fee to use any service?
Most certificates are free. Where a fee applies (business licences, passports), it's shown clearly before you submit.
